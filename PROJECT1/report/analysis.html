A Statistical Study of USD/INR Currency Exchange Rate¶

Scope of the study¶

To Understand the USD/INR exchnage rate through statistical application.

Data Source Used¶

RBI Daily interbank forex rates (USD vis-a-vis INR) Period:(08-08-2000 – 30-06-2026).

Methodology¶

This study uses quantitative descriptive statistical methods to examine historical movements and volatility in the USD/INR exchange rate. Pandas, Numpy, Matplotlib, Statsmodels, scipy, seaborn python libraries are used for analysis and visualisation.

Annual mean and median: Both measures show a broad increase in the exchange-rate level across the study period. Their similar trajectories support the conclusion that the long-term rise in USD/INR is not confined to the annual mean alone.

Annual standard deviation of the exchange-rate level: This measure varies across years, with relatively large values around 2008-2009, 2011–2013. It describes dispersion in exchange-rate levels within each year, not volatility in daily percentage returns. It should therefore be interpreted separately from the rolling-volatility graph.

Annual mean daily percentage change: The annual averages fluctuate around zero, Positive values indicate an average increase in USD/INR across observed daily changes, while negative values indicate an average decrease. These averages describe direction and average magnitude but do not capture the full distribution of daily movements.

Annual standard deviation of daily percentage changes: This measure indicates how variable daily percentage changes were within each year. The higher values around 2008–2013 suggest greater daily variability during that period, while lower values in some later years indicate comparatively subdued variability.

Findings¶

The USD/INR exchange rate exhibited a persistent long-term upward trend between 2000 and 2026, indicating a gradual depreciation of the Indian rupee against the US dollar. Annual statistical analysis reveals changes in the exchange rate across different years, while daily return distributions show that most movements were relatively small, with occasional significant fluctuations. Historical probability analysis found that daily depreciation exceeding 1% occurred 105 times (1.69%), while appreciation exceeding 1% occurred 81 times (1.30%). Overall, the analysis indicates that the rupee experienced a sustained weakening trend, accompanied by periods of varying volatility and occasional extreme daily movements.

Limitation¶

  • Missing observations and the long study period may affect comparability
  • The analysis describes historical patterns but does not establish their causes or predict future exchange-rate movements, hence further macroeconomic study is required.
